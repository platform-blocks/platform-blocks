The Carousel component displays a series of content in a horizontal scrollable view with optional navigation dots and controls.

Accessibility: the carousel is a labelled region (`accessibilityLabel`, default "Carousel"), each page a group named "n of N", the arrows are "Previous slide" / "Next slide", and the dots are "Go to slide n" buttons with the current one marked. With `autoPlay`, a pause / play button is always shown; autoplay pauses while the carousel is hovered, focused or touched, and stays off under the user's reduced-motion preference until they press play.
